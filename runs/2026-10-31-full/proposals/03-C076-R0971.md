## Eliminating Residual Datum Offsets Between MGI 1901 and ETRS89 in UrbanVizion's DataBridge Pipeline

**Company:** UrbanVizion d.o.o., NTP Novi Sad [C076] 
**Faculty:** Faculty of Technical Sciences, University of Novi Sad, Department of Civil Engineering and Geodesy 
**Researcher:** Katarina Đorđević (Катарина Ђорђевић), Associate Professor [R0971] 
**Company contact:** the company's in-house GIS specialist and one senior software engineer, to be named [C076] 
**Suggested duration:** 6 months, January–June 2027

---

### The problem

UrbanVizion's DataBridge pipeline transforms Serbian cadastral data from the MGI 1901 Balkans datum to ETRS89 using GDAL/Proj with standard published parameters and a national grid shift file obtained from the Serbian geodetic authority [C076-CH3]. In some areas—particularly those with older cadastral records—residual offsets of up to three metres remain after transformation [C076-CH3]. Three metres is enough to place a building footprint on the wrong side of a parcel boundary, creating errors in planning reviews for the company's Slovenian and Austrian municipal clients [C076-CH3]. The company does not yet know whether the remaining error originates from incorrect or incomplete transformation parameters, from inconsistencies in the source cadastral data, or from a combination [C076-CH3]. UrbanVizion has one GIS specialist and can dedicate one senior software engineer to the investigation for up to six months [C076].

Đorđević derived a locally refined NTv2-compatible grid shift model for the Bačka and Srem subregions of northern Serbia from 412 dual-system geodetic points, achieving a planimetric RMSE of 6.2 cm on withheld validation points—2.4 times better than the three-parameter Helmert solution used in national practice [SYN-PUB-10169]. She led the transformation infrastructure work package in the CADASTRANS cross-border project, which produced a shared transformation grid for the Hungary–Serbia border zone and tested it in PROJ and QGIS [SYN-PRJ-0468]. She also built a Python/GDAL rule-based tool for detecting silent CRS metadata errors in public dataset repositories [SYN-PUB-10173]. Those three bodies of work map directly onto UrbanVizion's open problem.

---

### Research questions

1. In which geographic subregions and cadastral record vintages do residual offsets after the standard MGI 1901→ETRS89 transformation exceed 0.5 m, and what is the spatial pattern of those residuals across the three anonymised municipal datasets UrbanVizion can provide?
2. Does applying Đorđević's published NTv2 grid [SYN-PUB-10169], or an updated version derived from additional control points in the affected areas, reduce the maximum residual offset below 0.3 m—the threshold at which a building footprint stays on the correct side of a 0.5 m cadastral boundary tolerance?
3. Can a lightweight automated check—similar in design to the CRS metadata detector in [SYN-PUB-10173]—flag likely datum-inconsistency errors in incoming cadastral files before they enter the DataBridge transformation step, and what false-positive rate does it produce on the company's existing dataset archive?

**Đorđević** brings the NTv2 grid shift model and least-squares collocation methodology for northern Serbia [SYN-PUB-10169]; experience designing and running cross-border CRS transformation infrastructure [SYN-PRJ-0468]; a working Python/GDAL pipeline for CRS error detection [SYN-PUB-10173]; access to GNSS reference data and FME Workbench licences in her lab [R0971]; and informal advisory contacts with several municipal cadastral offices in Vojvodina [R0971].

### Why this pair

The match is direct. She built the CRS metadata error detector in Python using the same GDAL/OGR stack that UrbanVizion's DataBridge pipeline runs on [SYN-PUB-10173]. She has run transformation infrastructure work in a project that required integration with PROJ and QGIS under real cross-border data conditions [SYN-PRJ-0468]. UrbanVizion's six-month, clearly scoped commitment fits the shorter engagement Đorđević has said is more feasible given her current PhD supervision load [R0971]. The company's GIS specialist and the anonymised municipal datasets give Đorđević a concrete applied testbed she does not currently have access to.
