## Correcting Extended-Size Grading for Indigo Atelier Studio Using Parametric Rules and 3D Virtual Fit

**Company:** Indigo Atelier Studio d.o.o., Novi Sad **Faculty:** Faculty of Technical Sciences, University of Novi Sad, Department of Graphic Engineering and Design **Researcher:** Marija Čolić (Марија Чолић), Associate Professor **Company contact:** to be named by the company (pattern maker or production coordinator) **Duration:** January 2027 – December 2027 (12 months)

### The problem

Indigo Atelier Studio introduced an EU 34–52 size range in its autumn-winter 2023 collection. Of the 2,000 pieces produced, 34% were returned, with nearly every return note citing wrong fit [C035-P1]. The grading above EU 44 was extrapolated from smaller-size blocks rather than developed from fitted blocks on real bodies in the extended range [C035-P4]. Each return parcel from Germany or the Netherlands costs €6–8 in shipping alone, before re-inspection and repackaging [C035-CH1]. The company has CLO 3D available but uses it only in a limited way [C035], and it has no dedicated R&D staff [C035].

### Research questions

1. Which specific grading increments between EU 44 and EU 52 — measured as deviations in front waist length, abdomen protrusion, and waist-to-hip ratio — account for the fit failures in the five core affected styles?
2. Can parametric grading rules derived from anthropometric body shape categories reduce the predicted fit deviation, as measured by virtual fit assessment in CLO 3D against a set of digital body models representing the five proportional categories identified in the literature, to under 10 mm mean landmark error across the size range?
3. Does the revised grading, applied to a pre-production virtual sample of at least two styles, reduce the simulated ease and length discrepancies to within the company's stated tolerance before any physical sample is cut?

### Plan

| Quarter | Activity | Deliverable |

| Q2 2027 (Apr–Jun) | Parametric grading rules are drafted for each of the five styles using the five proportional body shape categories from [SYN-PUB-04023]; rules are implemented in CLO 3D as revised digital blocks; automatic landmarking pipeline [SYN-PUB-04021] is used to extract measurement points from representative digital body models | Draft grading rule tables per style; set of revised CLO 3D blocks |
| Q3 2027 (Jul–Sep) | Virtual fit simulation run on revised blocks against digital body models for all five styles; fit quality assessed at 24-point evaluation scheme; if the department's Vitus Smart XXL scanner [R0325] can be scheduled, a small convenience scan of 20–30 volunteer customers or staff in the EU 46–52 range is conducted to validate the digital model selection | Virtual fit assessment report; scanner session report (if conducted) |
| Q4 2027 (Oct–Dec) | Company pattern makers apply the finalised grading rules to two styles scheduled for the next run; Čolić's team reviews the resulting CLO 3D virtual samples and signs off; documented grading rule set is formatted for in-house use; project report written | Production-ready corrected graded patterns for two styles; grading rule manual the company can apply independently to future styles |

### What each side brings

**Indigo Atelier Studio:** Five pattern block files in Adobe Illustrator and CLO 3D for the affected styles; full return dataset from the autumn-winter 2023 run with customer-reported measurements where available; two pattern makers and the production coordinator available for defined tasks within the project without being pulled off active collection work [C035]; CLO 3D licence already in place [C035].

**Marija Čolić and the department:** Expertise in parametric grading and anthropometric size system construction [SYN-PRJ-0623]; body shape category data from 418 scanned plus-size women including shape indices and proportional category centroids [SYN-PUB-04023]; automatic landmarking pipeline for 3D scan processing [SYN-PUB-04021]; prior experience supporting small apparel manufacturers through CLO 3D adoption [SYN-PRJ-0624]; access to the department's Vitus Smart XXL body scanner subject to scheduling [R0325].

### Why this pair

Čolić's 2022 study defined five proportional body categories for women with bust circumference above 100 cm — the population segment where Indigo Atelier's grading fails — using cluster analysis on 14 shape indices including abdomen protrusion and waist-to-hip ratio [SYN-PUB-04023]. She coordinated a project that supported three small apparel manufacturers in Vojvodina in adopting 3D body scanning and CLO 3D for pre-production virtual sampling [SYN-PRJ-0624]. Separately, she has direct experience leading a twelve-month implementation project integrating scan-based made-to-measure workflows at a Vojvodina sportswear producer [R0325], which shows the pace of change a regional SME can manage. Indigo Atelier already uses CLO 3D and Adobe Illustrator [C035], so no new tooling needs to be introduced. The company's return data provides a concrete, measurable starting point that matches Čolić's preference for projects with a real data collection or product development dimension [R0325].

### Open questions for the first meeting

1. Can Indigo Atelier extract per-order customer measurements from returns, and if so, how complete is that data — enough to map against the five body shape categories from [SYN-PUB-04023]?
2. Which two of the five affected styles should be prioritised for the Q4 production-ready output, given the spring-summer 2028 production schedule?
3. Is a small volunteer scan session (EU 46–52) in Novi Sad feasible given the scanner's scheduling calendar, and would the company's existing customers or contacts be a realistic recruitment pool?

### Agreed up front (proposal)

**IP:** Corrected pattern blocks and grading rule tables are owned by Indigo Atelier Studio. The methodology and any anonymised anthropometric data collected during the project may be used by Čolić's group for academic publication, with company review of any manuscript before submission. No company-identifiable data is published without written consent.

**Publication:** One journal paper or conference contribution is anticipated, describing the parametric grading methodology and virtual fit validation approach. Indigo Atelier Studio is acknowledged; specific return rate figures or financial data are included only with the company's written agreement.

**Cost and funding:** The parties should agree at the first meeting whether to apply for a bilateral innovation voucher or NTP Novi Sad co-financing instrument to cover scanner session costs and researcher time, or whether the project proceeds on a direct contract basis.
